## Calm is not the same as empty

A calm interface can still hold substantial information. The difference is structure: related items are grouped, hierarchy is obvious, and the screen reveals detail when it becomes useful.

## Let the rhythm stay predictable

Consistency lowers the effort required to use a product. Actions should appear in familiar places, labels should keep the same meaning, and navigation should behave predictably across devices.

- Use space to reveal relationships.
- Reserve strong color for meaningful emphasis.
- Write labels that describe an outcome.
- Keep motion short and purposeful.

## Respect attention

Notifications and warnings should earn the interruption they create. Routine confirmation can remain quiet, while genuinely important changes deserve stronger emphasis.

The best digital workspace does not demand attention for itself. It helps people direct attention toward their work.
